# Reporting time and savings

Three recorded base-cleaning runs took **9.561, 9.833 and 9.508 seconds**: an average of **9.6 seconds**. These are computer runtimes for cleaning, checks, database creation and base reports.

They exclude export retrieval, human review, the additional financial/scenario step, dashboard publication and delivery. They are not a measure of staff time saved.

## How to measure the benefit

1. Time the same report prepared manually.
2. Time hands-on work with the repeatable process, including review and exceptions.
3. Repeat across several comparable runs.
4. Subtract automated-process human effort from manual human effort.
5. Multiply by reports per year; deduct setup and ongoing support.

Example only: 120 minutes before and 20 minutes after releases 100 minutes per run, or 86.7 hours over 52 runs. At an assumed $35/hour, that is $3,033 of annual capacity before setup and support. **This has not been achieved or measured in this project.** It is not automatically a cash saving.

Current supported claim: “Built a repeatable reporting process with 34 base checks and financial reconciliations across 72 branch-months.” Do not claim client revenue growth, retention gains or measured staff savings without evidence.
