# My recording script

I aim for 1–2 minutes. I start on Summary with 2025 and all branches selected.

| Time | What I show | What I say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12 seconds | Headline and cards | “I built this report to explain why operating profit fell 21% in 2025, and which actions deserve attention first. I keep service revenue, cash receipts and unpaid bills separate.” |
| 12–32 seconds | Revenue waterfall; hover Root canal | “Root-canal services fell 26%, explaining 55% of the revenue decline. Average charges were broadly steady. I would check referrals, dentist availability and unbooked accepted plans. Recovering half the lost services is estimated ~$48K CAD/year in revenue before extra costs.” |
| 32–51 seconds | Lead-time chart; hover 30+ days | “No-shows rise from 3.0% for short-lead bookings to 9.3% for long-lead bookings. I would pilot a 7-day reminder and a 48-hour confirmation at Maple. The group opportunity is estimated ~$57K CAD/year in revenue. I would track no-shows and refilled slots.” |
| 51–69 seconds | Aging chart; hover 91–180 days | “estimated ~$447K CAD is unpaid for over 90 days. I would separate insurer, patient and billing-error work. Recovering 25% of the 91-to-180-day balances is estimated ~$17K CAD in one-off cash. This is not new revenue or extra profit.” |
| 69–96 seconds | Select Maple; drill through from its bar to Appointments; Back; open Patients and Finance | “The branch filter lets me target the pilot. I can inspect booking outcomes, patient activity and financial trends. In Finance, I drill from month to day to investigate changes. I return to Summary and clear the branch filter.” |
| 96–110 seconds | Ranked actions and measurement column | “I rank the actions by estimated value, account for overlapping reminder benefits, and separate one-off collections from recurring revenue. These are opportunities to test, not achieved savings.” |

I record at a readable zoom and pause briefly over tooltips. I rehearse the branch drill-through and date drill-down before recording. I save the recording as `docs/walkthrough-power-bi.mp4` after I build and validate the report.
